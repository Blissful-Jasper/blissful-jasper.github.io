Profile

I am a PhD candidate in marine science at Hohai University. My work focuses on tropical atmospheric dynamics, convectively coupled equatorial waves, tropical precipitation variability, and the evaluation of these processes in climate models.

I also use numerical models and machine learning tools to support meteorological data analysis, precipitation nowcasting, and reproducible scientific workflows.

Research interests

  • Convectively coupled equatorial waves and moist dynamics
  • Tropical precipitation variability over oceans
  • CMIP6 and climate model diagnostics
  • WRF, satellite data, and reproducible analysis workflows
  • Deep learning applications in meteorology

Education

  • PhD candidate in Ocean Science, Hohai University
  • Master's study in Physical Oceanography, Hohai University
  • Bachelor's study in Ocean Technology, Jiangsu Ocean University
